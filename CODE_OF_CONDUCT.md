# Code of Conduct

Monkeys Memory is an open-source project for people building better developer
tools together. We expect participation to be respectful, constructive, and
focused on improving the product.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent, and clarify when something is unclear.
- Keep criticism specific to ideas, code, docs, or behavior.
- Respect privacy. Do not share real team memory, secrets, or customer data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Sharing exploit details or sensitive data in public issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; or restrict
participation when behavior harms the project or community.

If you need to report conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through a
private channel.
